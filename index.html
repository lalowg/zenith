01Sessions

Four guided sessions, one for each kind of evening.

Every session has a fixed rhythm and a handful of quiet cues that appear beneath the sun as you breathe. Pick the one that matches the day you have had.

  1. I

    Arrive

    For the first minutes after a loud day. The out-breath is longer than the in-breath, and there is nothing else to do.

    Length
    3 min
    Rhythm
    In 4 · Out 6
  2. II

    Clear

    Two minutes between one task and the next. A short pause at the top of each breath, like looking up from the page.

    Length
    2 min
    Rhythm
    In 4 · Hold 2 · Out 4
  3. III

    Steady

    An even square of breath for when thoughts crowd one another. Four sides, four counts each, drawn slowly.

    Length
    5 min
    Rhythm
    4 · 4 · 4 · 4
  4. IV

    Unwind

    For the edge of sleep. A long hold and a longer release. If the hold feels like effort, shorten it in Your rhythm.

    Length
    8 min
    Rhythm
    In 4 · Hold 7 · Out 8

02The arc of a breath

Every breath is a small day.

The landscape is not decoration. Each of its parts answers one movement of the breath, so you can stop counting and simply watch.

In-breath

The rise

The sun lifts out of the lake and climbs the meridian. The sky warms toward peach, and a dot travels the first part of the arc. Breathe in through the nose, unhurried, as if the light were pulling you up with it.

Hold

The zenith

At the top of the breath the sun rests at its highest point. Haze gathers on the horizon and the light grows a little brighter. Keep the shoulders soft. A hold is a pause, never a strain.

Out-breath

The descent

The sun returns to the water and the sky cools toward lilac. A breeze moves across the lake and the reflection loosens into ripples. Let the breath leave slowly, a little longer than it came in.

Rest

The stillness

With the lungs empty, the water turns to glass. When a breath is complete, a star appears above the hills. By the end of a session they form a constellation, with the last star at the zenith.

03Rhythms

A small library of shapes for the breath.

Choosing a rhythm loads it into Your rhythm above, where you can still change every count.

10s

Even

In 5 · Out 5

The simplest shape: as long in as out, about six breaths a minute. A good place to begin if you are new to this.

10s

Long out-breath

In 4 · Out 6

The out-breath is given a little more room than the in-breath. Many people find this the most settling shape of all.

12s

Triangle

In 4 · Hold 4 · Out 4

Three equal sides with a pause at the top only. Steady, but with no empty hold at the bottom.

16s

Square

In 4 · Hold 4 · Out 4 · Rest 4

Four equal sides. Counting them gives a busy mind a small, orderly job to do.

19s

Four, seven, eight

In 4 · Hold 7 · Out 8

A long hold and a long release, often practised at night. If the hold is uncomfortable, use the pace control to make it quicker.

14s

Your rhythm

In 4 · Hold 2 · Out 6 · Rest 2

Whatever you set in the dock lives here. The line redraws as you move the sliders, and it is remembered on this device.

04Before you begin

Sit as if you were the horizon: level, unhurried, wide.

  1. Settle

    Sit or lie where you will not be disturbed for a few minutes. Let the floor or the chair take your weight.

  2. Soften

    Unclench the jaw. Let the shoulders fall away from the ears. Rest the tongue behind the top teeth.

  3. Follow

    Watch the sun rather than the numbers. If you lose the count, wait for the next sunrise and join it there.

  4. Return

    When the session ends, stay a moment under the stars. Then let the breath find its own rhythm again.

A note on care

ZENITH is a place to practise slow breathing. It is not a treatment and it does not diagnose or cure anything. If you feel light-headed, tingly or short of air, stop and breathe naturally. If you are pregnant or live with a heart or breathing condition, skip the holds and talk to a qualified professional before trying breath-hold rhythms.

05The sanctuary

The zenith is the point directly above you. Wherever you stand, it is yours.

  • No streaks to keep. Missing a day is not a failure. The landscape is the same tomorrow.
  • No scores to beat. Breaths are counted only so you can see where you are in a session.
  • No feed to return to. When the session ends, there is nothing more to scroll.
